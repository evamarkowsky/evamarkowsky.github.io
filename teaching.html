Teaching

I teach quantitative methods at all levels, from introductory statistics to graduate seminars on machine learning and text analysis. All courses emphasise understanding over black-box application and work with real research data.

University of Potsdam

Graduate (M.Sc. Economic Policy and Quantitative Methods)

Text Analysis and Machine Learning Methods for Economic Research

Seminar, in English · Summer terms since 2025

Quantitative text analysis for economic research: text preprocessing, supervised classification, clustering, word embeddings, topic models (LDA/STM), and an introduction to transformer models and LLMs. Students work with real corpora such as parliamentary speeches.

Machine Learning with R

Seminar, in English · Winter term 2025/26 (new)

Supervised and unsupervised ML methods for economists: regularisation, tree-based methods, model evaluation, and ML for causal inference. Hands-on implementation in the tidymodels ecosystem; assessed via an independent project with real economic data.

Undergraduate (B.Sc. Economics, Politics & Economics, and others)

Einführung in die Statistik

Lecture, in German · Summer terms since 2025

Large introductory statistics course serving seven degree programmes across three faculties. Covers descriptive statistics, probability, estimation, and hypothesis testing. Uses live polling (PINGO) and real-world examples drawn from election data, sports statistics, and a student-generated survey.

Vertiefende Methoden der Ökonometrie

Lecture, in German · Winter terms since 2024/25

Advanced regression methods, panel data, time series, and causal inference (IV/2SLS, RDD). Practical implementation in Stata and R.
