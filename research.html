PRIMA – Primary Data Meta-Analyses on Mechanisms of Efficient Competition

(with M Beblo, C Gardiner)

We build a harmonised individual-level dataset from experimental studies on competitive entry to analyse the efficiency of self-selection into competition and the causal effect of competitive pressure on performance, separately by gender.

Work in Progress

The Language of Competition: Does the Language of Instruction Shape Competitive Preferences of Women and Men?

(with H Fornwagner, M Beblo)

Using the PRIMA data infrastructure, we investigate whether gender differences in competitive entry vary with the language and gendered framing of experimental instructions.
